Is AgentGo an ERP system?
AgentGo is more than an ERP. It combines ERP, AI customer service, and intelligent product listing in one travel operations platform.

Yes. AgentGo supports API integration with existing websites and business systems.

Does AgentGo support multiple languages?
Yes. AgentGo supports multilingual customer service and travel product management.
